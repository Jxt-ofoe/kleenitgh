Frequently Asked Questions

What types of disinfectants do you use in healthcare facilities?

We use hospital-grade, broad-spectrum virucidal and bactericidal disinfectants proven against common clinical pathogens.

How do you prevent cross-contamination between treatment rooms?

We implement strict color-coded microfiber cloths, separate mop heads per room, and mandatory single-use glove replacements.

Can your team clean medical offices after operating hours?

Yes, we routinely perform evening and night-shift clinical sanitization to ensure patient appointments are never disturbed.

Are your cleaners trained in bio-safety protocols?

Yes, our healthcare cleaning personnel receive specialized training in PPE compliance, chemical dwell times, and bio-waste protocols.
